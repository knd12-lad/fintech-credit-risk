Model Specifications

Trained Random Forest Classifier

RiskWise utilizes a supervised Random Forest ensemble trained on a verified lending benchmark dataset comprising 32,409 historical loan records. The classifier optimizes Gini impurity across 200 decision trees to produce calibrated probability outputs.

  • Ensemble Architecture: 200 estimators, max depth 16, min samples leaf 2
  • Deterministic Preprocessing: StandardScaler for numerical values, One-Hot & Ordinal encoding
  • Audited Persistence: Model weights and preprocessing pipelines are serialised together

300–900 Score & Risk Bands

The predicted default probability $P(\text{default})$ is mapped linearly into an intuitive 300 to 900 scoring scale:

Credit Score = round(900 - (Probability × 600))
Low Risk: 780 – 900
Default Prob < 20%
Medium Risk: 600 – 779
Default Prob 20% – 50%
High Risk: 300 – 599
Default Prob ≥ 50%
Why This Score? (Exact Shapley)

RiskWise computes exact Shapley values across all 1,024 feature coalitions averaged over 8 baseline reference applicants. Each input is converted to score points ($\text{impact\_points} = \text{round}(-\phi_i \times 600)$), showing the exact degree to which income, rate, or grade helped or hurt your score.

Model Limitations

The machine learning model was trained on a public lending dataset denominated in US dollars and has not been validated on Indian borrowers or localized domestic credit parameters. Age is an input in the historical dataset, and all outputs, scores, and probabilities generated by RiskWise are statistical model estimates intended exclusively for demonstrative and educational use.
